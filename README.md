# Physics 20 Study Suite

A single-file study app covering all 8 chapters of Alberta Physics 20
(Units A–D). Open `index.html` directly in any browser — no build step or
server needed. All progress is saved in `localStorage`.

## Study modes

- **Flashcards** — 124 terms across all 8 chapters, with a 3D flip,
  swipe/keyboard navigation, "got it / still learning" rating, and a
  per-chapter filter.
- **Definitions test** — multiple choice. Each term is shown with its correct
  definition plus three plausible distractors pulled from the same chapter.
  Tracks accuracy per chapter.
- **Equation test** — one chapter at a time, 78 original practice problems
  (8–10 per chapter). Enter a numeric answer (decimal or scientific notation,
  e.g. `1.07e-8`); answers are checked within ~3% to allow for rounding, then
  a full worked solution is revealed (formula → substitution → answer).
- **Cumulative test** — a 22-question mixed unit test pulling equation and
  definition questions from every chapter, with a final score and a
  chapter-by-chapter breakdown.
- **Reference** — the Physics 20 formula sheet and constants, organized by
  chapter, for looking things up mid-test.
- **Dashboard** — mastery per chapter across flashcards, definitions, and
  equation tests, plus a single button to reset all progress.

## Design

Neutral off-white / charcoal palette with one muted slate accent, the Inter
typeface, generous spacing, clear hover/tap states on every control, and
consistent fade-and-slide transitions across all modes. Responsive from
phone to desktop.

## Curriculum coverage

1. Motion in One Direction
2. Vectors & Motion in Two Dimensions
3. Forces & Newton's Laws
4. Gravitation
5. Circular Motion
6. Work & Energy
7. Oscillatory Motion
8. Mechanical Waves

`Physics20_OscillationsWaves_StudyGuide.docx` is the companion glossary,
practice test, and answer key for chapters 7 and 8.

## Notes

All practice problems use original numbers and scenarios written to test the
listed formulas; they are not copied from any textbook.
